- Native **Open at Login** setting, with system approval guidance and visible errors.
- Sparkle-powered Check for Updates, daily automatic checks, and optional automatic installation; signed update archives and feeds.
- Installed version and build pinned in Settings, plus a Quit action.
- Liquid Glass navigation and Settings footer on macOS 26, with system appearance and material fallbacks.
- Improved settings typography and space, accessible switches and icon buttons, and keyboard-activatable screenshot tiles.
- Universal macOS releases with tag validation, CI tests, signature verification, and checksums.
